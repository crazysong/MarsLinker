Privacy Policy for MarsConnect
Effective Date: July 30, 2026

Last Updated: July 30, 2026

MarsConnect ("we," "our," or "the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data handling when you use the MarsConnect browser extension.

1. Zero Data Collection and Zero Uploads
MarsConnect does NOT collect, store, or upload any user data.

No Access to Personal Data: The Extension does not read, extract, or monitor your browsing history, web contents, personal credentials, cookies, or clipboard data.

No Server Uploads: The Extension does not transmit or upload any information to MarsEad servers or any third-party servers.

2. Functionality and Data Flow
MarsConnect functions exclusively as a dedicated data receiver for MarsPub.

The Extension operates strictly as a one-way receiver to accept local instructions and payload dispatched from MarsPub.

All data processing occurs transiently and locally within your browser instance.

Once the operation is complete, any temporary in-memory data is immediately cleared with zero permanent storage.

3. Permissions Usage
Any browser permissions requested by MarsConnect are used solely to facilitate local communication and instruction execution from MarsPub. We do not use permissions to collect, analyze, or track user activity under any circumstances.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics
MarsConnect:

Contains no analytics tools, tracking scripts, or telemetry code.

Is entirely ad-free.

Does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.
